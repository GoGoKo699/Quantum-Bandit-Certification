# Scientific reading route

A common certificate against every better-reward state has the classical spectral rate. The same rate controls how often an adaptive learner must pull each suboptimal arm, even if it retains every quantum specimen.

1. [Model and exact claims](model.md): known reference, one fixed IID alternative, the two error probabilities and the classical rate.
2. [Uniform certification](certification.md): support completion, a feasible shared-phase mixture, the strong converse, finite-confidence spectral tests and endpoint cases.
3. [Modular mixtures](modular_mixtures.md): the exact measured-divergence identity and logarithmic regularized Umegaki gap.
4. [Fixed-block measurements](block_measurements.md): why a better block would contradict uniform certification after repetition.
5. [Adaptive regret](adaptive_regret.md): stopped measurement effects, a fixed finite family of replacement arms, and the exact exploration coefficient.
6. [Worked qubit examples](examples.md): a finite-copy decision and a two-arm instance with a strictly stronger converse than the Umegaki bound.
7. [Interpretation](interpretation.md) and [resources](resources.md): the proof mechanism and implementation costs.

The [related-work account](../references/RELATED_WORK.md) identifies the inherited ingredients. The [verification guide](../verification/README.md) distinguishes numerical checks from the analytical proofs.
